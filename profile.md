The scorer sends this whole file to the model as the description of who it is scoring for, so replace it with your own.
Plain prose works best: say what you build, what you want, and what rules a posting out.

**Jane Doe** - B.S. Computer Science, graduating May 2028.

## What she actually builds

Backend services and data pipelines in Python and Go.

## Stack

## Prior roles

One software engineering internship at a mid-size company, one research assistant role.

## Fit rules

**Strong fit**: backend, infrastructure, ML systems, and LLM/agent engineering internships.
A generalist "Software Engineer Intern" at a strong company is a first-choice target (80+).
Company quality and engineering content decide the score; an AI-flavored title is a small bonus, never a requirement.

**Solid fit (around 70)**: a generalist software engineering internship at a smaller but credible company, even when the title gives no detail.
A generic title is missing information, not negative information.

**Weak fit**: pure frontend or design, product management, IT support, sales or solutions engineering, data analyst and BI roles, and anything requiring 3+ years of experience.

Hard gates (score 15 or below): roles that require an in-progress PhD, and cycles other than the one above.
